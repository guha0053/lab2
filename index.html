About Me

My photography journey

One of my earliest memories is always wanting to take photographs with my parents' simple camera. In the year of 91, I mostly just wanted to press the shutter button, which resulted in hundreds of photographs of grass and the ground. Looking back, I guess I was already experimenting with photography from a very young age.

In high school, I took a photography course, and later I continued studying photography at university. One experience during my university studies really kickstarted my passion for photography.

For a photography assignment focused on clothing and fashion, we broke into an old abandoned factory to create our own photoshoot. I was in charge of the shoot, experimenting with flashes and different lighting setups while working with professional models.

That experience showed me how much I enjoy combining creativity, lighting and photography to create something unique. It made me realize that photography was something I wanted to continue pursuing. Perhaps all time to the age of 91.

My ambition

Take the perfect shot

My goal is to continue developing my photography skills and eventually be able to make a living from it. I would also love to create an image that stands out and gets recognised in a competition such as Picture of the Year (Årets Bild).

For me, that would be a great way of knowing that all the time, creativity and experimentation I have put into photography has resulted in something truly meaningful.
